title=Show Healing Progress
description=Shows how far each wound has healed in the Health tab, under the body part.
description=Ex: "- Fracture: 45% healed"
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Works for scratches, lacerations, deep wounds, bites, burns, fractures and stitches. A body part with several wounds gets one line per wound.
description=[*]When a wound is healed, the line stays at 100% while a bandage, stitches or a splint is still on the body part, so you know it can come off. Once you take it off, the line goes away.
description=[*]Stitches show 100% once they can be removed safely. Removing them earlier reopens the deep wound.
description=[*]A deep wound that stopped healing says why: it needs a bandage, or there is still glass inside.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Light on performance: one quick check per in-game minute, and every frame only while the Health tab is open.
description=
description=Works in SP.
description=Should work in MP, but it was not tested.
description=Can be added to existing saves. Wounds you already have show an estimate (~) because the mod did not see how bad they were at the start.
description=
description=Does not replace game files.
